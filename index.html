A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers about HypnoMind sessions, training and learning options.

What is hypnotherapy?

Hypnotherapy uses guided relaxation and focused attention to support personal exploration and positive change. Each person's experience is different, and results are not guaranteed.

Do you offer online and offline sessions?

Yes. HypnoMind offers online and offline options, subject to availability and suitability. Share your preference in the enquiry form and we can discuss the details.

What kind of sessions can I enquire about?

You can enquire about personal hypnotherapy, Past Life Regression as an exploratory practice, Inner Child Work and NLP-based personal-development sessions.

Can beginners learn hypnotherapy at HypnoMind?

Yes. Training enquiries are welcome from beginners as well as learners looking to develop their existing skills. The learning path can be discussed according to your goals.

Is training available one-to-one or in a group?

Both one-to-one and group training formats may be available. Online and offline learning options can also be discussed when you enquire.

Will I receive a certificate after training?

A certificate is available on completion of the applicable training programme. Please confirm the programme requirements and completion criteria before enrolling.

How do I book a session or ask about training?

Use the Enquire Now form on this page, choose the service or training you are interested in, and share your preferred mode. The HypnoMind team can then get in touch with you.

Please note: Hypnotherapy and personal-development sessions are not a substitute for medical diagnosis or treatment.
